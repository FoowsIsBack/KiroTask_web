Organize Your Life
with KiroTask

The simple yet powerful task manager that helps you stay on top of everything. Plan, track, and accomplish your goals effortlessly.

Everything You Need to Stay Organized

Powerful features designed to help you manage tasks, stay focused, and achieve more every day.

Calendar task Management

Add, view, and manage tasks per day using the built-in calendar. Days with tasks are color-coded for quick scanning.

Edit & notes support

Each task supports a note field. Swipe left or tap edit to update task title and notes anytime.

Progress tracking

See your daily and monthly completion rate with visual progress bars and a done/total task counter.
